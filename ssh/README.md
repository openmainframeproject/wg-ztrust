# ztrust/ssh

This is the ztrust/ssh folder with SSH public keys of members
of the z/OS and z/VM and z/Linux communities.

SSH keys are of limited use at this time in this project.
Their presence here is primarily for consistency with the other
symmetric+asymmetric cryptographic ecosystems represented.

There are some people who have presented their SSH public key
when they were asked to share their PGP public key. Don't do that.
It's not harmful, but it's completely ineffective. SSH and GPG and PKI
all use the same combination of symmetric and asymmetric cryptography
and have similar "public/private" concepts, but SSH (and its keys) is
not used for signing files nor for encrypting email or web traffic.
