## The Demise of eMudhra

The eMudhra Certificate Authority has been in recent news.
Evidently, a logic flaw (a bug) in their certificate handling resulted
in intermediate certificates with a too-short asymmetric key pair
which was easily compromised.

It appears that the Certificate Authority business was only a small part
of eMudhra total, so they are abandoning their CA customers. This impact
is heavy on those with eMudhra-issued certificates.
