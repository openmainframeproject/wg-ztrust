## To QR or not to QR

Rick reported via email that his new company business card would have
a QR code with his PGP fingerprint. (Not a URL and not the actual key.)
Berry responded to that email at length discussing the challenges
of using QR codes. The group discussed pros and cons. The majority
seemed to feel that plain hexadecimal is a better starting point.

Matt pointed out that services like WKD require owner access to web
space which is problematic for PGP participants with email addresses
in domains such as GMail.com.

Lyz shared experience that people seeing her PGP fingerprint on her
business card has led to potentially better first impressions.
(People are impressed and recognize technical competence.)
